Laminate Bench

Type a stacking sequence and see each ply, its fiber direction, and how the laminate's in-plane stiffness changes with loading angle. Everything is computed live with classical lamination theory.

Examples: [0/90]2s, [±30/0]s, 0,45,-45,90. Use ± for paired plies and a trailing s for symmetric.

View on a headset

On Meta Quest, turn on hand tracking in Settings, Movement Tracking, then open this page in the Quest browser. The laminate appears half a metre in front of you with a keyboard below it. Press keys with your index fingertip to type a new layup, then press Build. Pinch both hands away from the laminate at once to bring everything back in front.
